The Westminster Mysteries, by Sarah Lustig

Theo Duncan is just an ordinary student. Except he also happens to be the son of the Prime Minister. With his friend Sammy, he solves the mysteries that baffle Parliament.

Signed copiesTable S52, Oxford

Book one

Mystery in the Palace of Westminster

When the parliamentary mace is stolen from inside the Houses of Parliament, Theo is determined to help his dad get it back. But he can't do it alone. And when help is offered, there's a problem. It comes from the new girl at school, Sammy Jhor, who's a supporter of the opposition party.

Paperback, 272 pages. Highly Commended, Selfies Children's Book Award 2023.

Signed copy, £7.99

Book two

Body in the Thames

While Theo and Sammy grapple with work experience inside Parliament, Westminster is shaken by the discovery of a body in the Thames. All signs point to murder. With security heightened, they must be especially clever to evade detection as they question MPs and search the Palace for clues.

Paperback, 288 pages. Shortlisted, Selfies Book Awards 2025.

Signed copy, £7.99

Book three, the finale, out now

Murder at Speaker's House

An arsonist sets fire to one of the grandest parts of Parliament, Speaker's House, and this time somebody is dead. Everyone suspects the radical Novembrists. But is someone framing them to thwart the referendum? To find out, Theo and Sammy have to go undercover and put their own lives on the line.

Paperback, 304 pages. Published 18 September 2026.

Hello, I'm Sarah

I grew up in London and went to school in Westminster, with politicians' children. My experiences at school and my interest in politics gave me the idea for the Westminster Mysteries.

I came late to reading for myself. I didn't fall back in love with books until I was about twelve, in the school library, with The Ruby in the Smoke by Philip Pullman. I've wanted to be a writer ever since.

I've been a book editor for nearly 15 years, and now live in Buckinghamshire, reading, writing and pottering on my balcony garden.
